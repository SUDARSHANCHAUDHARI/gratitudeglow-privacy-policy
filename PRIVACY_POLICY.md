# GratitudeGlow Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 17, 2026
_Last updated: October 19, 2026_
**Version:** 1.2.0

GratitudeGlow ("we," "our," or "us") is an Android app. GratitudeGlow is a gratitude journaling app for Android that stores your journal entries only on your device. It has no accounts and no cloud sync. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores, how it is used, and the choices you have. By installing or using GratitudeGlow you agree to the practices described below.

## Information We Collect

### Journal Entries
- The journal entries you write are stored on your device. Each entry holds a title, the entry text, an optional mood, optional tags, and the dates it was created and last edited.
- We do not receive or collect your journal entries.

### Account Information
- GratitudeGlow has no sign-in and no user accounts. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or any other account details.

### Location and Device Information
- GratitudeGlow does not collect location data.
- GratitudeGlow does not collect device identifiers, usage analytics, or crash reports.

## How We Use Your Information

- Your journal entries are used only inside the app, to show, edit, and delete your entries.
- We do not sell your journal content.
- We do not use your journal content for advertising.

## Storage and Retention

### Data Storage
- Journal entries are stored in a local database inside the app's private storage on your device.
- The app does not upload your entries to any server.

### Backups
- The app turns off Android cloud backup, so the app does not back up your entries to your Google account.
- If you move to a new phone with a direct device-to-device transfer, Android may copy app data according to its default behaviour.

### Data Retention
- Entries stay on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
- We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain.

### Data Security
- Local data is protected by Android app sandboxing.
- You should protect your device with a screen lock.

## Data Sharing

### Third Parties
- GratitudeGlow does not share your data with anyone.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.
- The app is distributed through Google Play, which has its own terms and privacy policy.

We do **not** share your data with:
- Data brokers
- Advertising networks
- Any other third party

## Permissions Used

### Required Permissions

* **INTERNET**: Declared in the app manifest. The current version of the app does not make network requests and does not send your journal entries over the internet.

### Optional Permissions

* None. The current version does not request notification, location, camera, contacts, or storage permissions, and it does not send reminder notifications.

## Your Rights and Controls

### Managing Your Entries
- You can view, edit, and delete your journal entries in the app.

### Data Access
- All of your data is on your device and visible in the app. We hold no copy of it.

You can also contact us using the methods in the Contact Us section.

### Permissions

## Security

## Data Deletion

GratitudeGlow has no accounts, and we hold no copy of your data. All of your data is on your device, and you can delete it yourself.

### Delete a Single Entry
1. Open GratitudeGlow
2. Open the entry, or find it in the list on the home screen
3. Tap delete and confirm

### Delete All Data
- **Clear app data:** Android Settings -> Apps -> GratitudeGlow -> Storage -> Clear storage
- **Uninstall:** uninstalling GratitudeGlow removes all of its entries from your device

### What Gets Deleted
- Deleted entries are removed from the app's local database at once.
- Clearing app data or uninstalling removes every entry.
- We cannot recover deleted entries, because we hold no copy.
